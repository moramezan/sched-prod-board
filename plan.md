# sched-prod incident board — plan

The on-call board for sched-prod. One page, one list: tonight's incidents, newest first, so on-call sees
what just broke and takes it.

## Flows

| flow | who | what happens |
|------|-----|--------------|
| `open-board` | on-call | opens the board; sees tonight's incidents |
| `ack-incident` | on-call | picks an incident on the board and acks it — "I've got this one" |
| `watch-board` | on-call | keeps the board open; the "N new" bar counts new incidents without a reload, and a click brings them in |

## Requirements

- **R1** The list is newest first and holds still while it is on screen: a refresh never moves, inserts above or
  removes a row on the board. New incidents wait behind a **"N new" bar**; clicking it brings them in at the top.
  *(Changed after step-1 evidence: a new incident landing on top moved rows under the pointer and Ack hit the wrong
  incident — recording 00:40, expected INC-4799, got INC-6878. Product decided: hold still + "N new" bar; Ack stays on the row.)*
- **R2** Each incident row has an **Ack** button. Ack sends the ack (`api.ack(id)`) and marks the row acked.
- **R3** Show a loader until incidents load.
- **R4** The board refreshes itself every 2 seconds while open.
- **R5** Times show in the viewer's local time; day before month for EU locales (dd/mm).
- **R6** The page names its build: `<meta name="build" content="<git sha>">` in the head and the same sha in the footer.

## Hooks (pinned — tests and the replay rely on them)

| `data-testid` | element |
|---------------|---------|
| `board` | the board's main area |
| `loader` | the loader (gone once incidents load) |
| `new-bar` | the "N new" bar (only visible when new incidents wait; its slot keeps its height so rows never shift) |
| `empty` | what shows when there are no incidents |
| `incident-list` | the list |
| `incident-row` | one incident row, with `data-id="<incident id>"` |
| `ack` | the Ack button in a row |
| `acked` | the acked mark in a row |
| `build-sha` | the sha in the footer |
